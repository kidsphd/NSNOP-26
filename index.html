01 · Programme

A School-Based Oral Health Model

NSNOP was designed as a connected oral-health platform rather than a series of isolated screening camps.

IMAGE 03
School outreach photograph

Taking Oral Health to the School

The programme connects children, schools, teachers, dental professionals, parents and the health system through a school-based pathway.

The model focuses on access, early detection, prevention, health promotion and continuity of care.

06 · Activities

More Than Screening

The programme combined clinical services with education, engagement and digital documentation.

🔎 Screening

Systematic oral-health screening of schoolchildren.

📚 Education

Structured, interactive oral-health education.

🛡️ Prevention

07 · Education

Learning Beyond the Dental Chair

Health education was delivered through creative and child-centred methods.

  • Skits – memorable oral-health messages through performance.
  • Poems – creative reinforcement of key messages.
  • Interactive games – participation and recall.
  • Demonstrations – practical oral-hygiene skills.
  • Question-and-answer – encouraging children to ask and respond.
  • Rewards and recognition – encouraging involvement.
Every Child – An Oral Health Kit

09 · Continuity

A Referral Is Not the End

Phase 2 strengthens the pathway from screening to comprehensive care.

IMAGE 14
Phase 2 treatment / follow-up

Phase 2 – The Next Step

The master dataset records 4,615 referrals, representing 80.9% of the screened population.

Phase 2 was initiated in August 2026 with greater emphasis on bringing referred children to the dental hospital for comprehensive oral care, subject to parent/guardian consent.

No child identified through NSNOP should be lost between screening and treatment.
